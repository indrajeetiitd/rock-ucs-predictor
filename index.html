How to read this. All numbers on the performance panel come from one stratified 80/20 train/test split (fixed seed 42) of the 1080-sample database, using the same protocol as the published RF-LR baseline (Liu et al., 2023, Rock Mech. Bull. 2, 100081). A different random split gives a different R².

Use within range. Models are trained on the observed ranges shown under each slider; predictions outside them are extrapolations. The ± band is 1.96 × the standard deviation of the test-set residuals of the selected model. This tool supports preliminary estimation and does not replace laboratory UCS testing.
